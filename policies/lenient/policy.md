# Meridian Bank SME Credit Policy — Growth Edition (v3.1)

## 1. Purpose and Scope
This policy governs underwriting of term loans to small and medium enterprises (SMEs) with requests up to USD 2,000,000. It applies to every application regardless of channel. Underwriters and automated assistants must apply it as written; discretion exists only where a rule below expressly provides for an exception.

## 2. Definitions
All ratios are calculated on the most recent fiscal year of financial statements provided by the applicant.
- **Debt service coverage ratio (DSCR)** = (net income + interest expense + depreciation) divided by (interest expense + current portion of long-term debt + the annual payment on the proposed loan). The proposed loan is amortised monthly at an assumed rate of 9.0% over the requested term.
- **Debt-to-equity ratio** = total liabilities divided by total equity.
- **Current ratio** = total current assets divided by total current liabilities.
- **Loan-to-revenue ratio** = requested loan amount divided by the most recent annual revenue.
- **Collateral coverage** = appraised collateral value divided by the requested loan amount.
- **Years in business** is measured from the date of incorporation to the application date.

## 3. Governance
The Credit Risk Committee reviews this policy annually. Deviations are logged in the exceptions register. This Growth Edition applies to branches designated as growth-oriented, with higher risk appetite.

## CP-1 Minimum Operating History
The applicant must have operated for at least 2 years. Applicants with less than 2 years in business fail this rule. No exceptions are permitted.

## CP-2 Prohibited Industries
The Bank does not lend to businesses whose primary activity is cannabis or adult entertainment. An applicant in either industry fails this rule. All other industries, including gambling, firearms and cryptocurrency, are permitted subject to the remaining rules.

## CP-3 Minimum Debt Service Coverage
The DSCR must be at least 1.15. A DSCR below 1.05 fails this rule. A DSCR from 1.05 up to (but not including) 1.15 is an exception, available only when collateral coverage is at least 1.20 (120%); in that case the case carries an exception to be referred to the Credit Committee. A DSCR in that range without collateral coverage of at least 1.20 fails this rule.

## CP-4 Maximum Leverage
The debt-to-equity ratio must not exceed 4.5. A ratio above 4.5, or negative equity, fails this rule.

## CP-5 Minimum Liquidity
The current ratio must be at least 1.00. A lower current ratio fails this rule.

## CP-6 Maximum Loan Size Relative to Revenue
The requested loan must not exceed 50% of the most recent annual revenue (loan-to-revenue of 0.50 or less). A higher ratio fails this rule.

## CP-7 Financial Statement Requirements
The applicant must provide financial statements (income statement and balance sheet) for 2 consecutive fiscal years. Fewer than 2 years of statements fails this rule.

## CP-8 Collateral
For requests above USD 400,000 collateral coverage must be at least 0.80 (80%). Requests of USD 400,000 or less have no collateral requirement under this rule. Insufficient coverage on a request above the threshold fails this rule.

## CP-9 Personal Guarantee
A personal guarantee from the principal owner is required only when the requested loan exceeds USD 250,000 or the business has been operating for fewer than 5 years. Otherwise no guarantee is required. A loan that requires a guarantee but lacks one fails this rule.

## CP-10 Sanctions Screening
The applicant and its principal owner must be screened against sanctions lists. Any confirmed match fails this rule and the application must be declined.

## CP-11 Adverse Media
Only adverse media findings of high severity concerning the applicant or its principal owner are an exception that must be referred to the Credit Committee. Low and medium severity findings are recorded but do not trigger an exception.

## CP-12 Acceptable Risk Grade
The internal risk grade (A best to E worst) must be A, B, C or D. A grade of E fails this rule.

## CP-13 Delegation of Authority
Approval authority depends on the requested amount. Loans up to USD 150,000 may be approved by an Analyst. Loans above USD 150,000 and up to USD 500,000 require a Senior Credit Officer. Loans above USD 500,000 require the Credit Committee. Any application carrying an exception under this policy also requires the Credit Committee regardless of amount.

## 14. Decision Rule
If any rule CP-1 through CP-12 fails, the recommendation is to decline. Otherwise, if any rule carries an exception, the recommendation is to refer the case to the Credit Committee. If required information is missing so that a rule cannot be assessed, the recommendation is to refer. Otherwise the recommendation is to approve.

## 15. Record Retention
Credit files are retained for 5 years after the loan is closed or declined.
